Recording consent — NexAI Interview

Before this interview begins, please read carefully.

If you proceed with proctoring enabled, this session will:

  • Record your webcam video continuously throughout the interview
  • Record your screen (you will be asked to grant permission)
  • Capture your microphone audio
  • Take a photograph of you at the start for identity purposes
  • Run AI behaviour analysis on the recording (multi-face detection, phone detection)

All recordings are stored on EnternsTech's GCP, with a retention period set by the legal team. AI flags are reviewed by a human recruiter — they are never used to auto-reject.

What we do NOT monitor (out of scope — specialist vendor or native app required):
• Lockdown browser (tab/copy blocking) — needs an installed native application
• Secondary device or hidden phone detection via audio/Wi-Fi — needs native + hardware
• Virtual machine blocking — needs native system access
• Government-ID biometric face matching — specialist paid identity service
• Keystroke-dynamics identity — low reliability, skipped
